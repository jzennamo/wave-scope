History

Training maxes

TM is the number every percentage is taken from. The app keeps it at or under 90% of your best set in the last six weeks (plain Epley) and raises it at most one step per wave: +10 squat and sumo, +5 bench.

Training block

Main lifts

Main lifts run on percentages of a training max and progress off the AMRAP set. Everything else is accessory work on double progression.
